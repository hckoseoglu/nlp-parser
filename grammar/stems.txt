# CMPE 561 Application Project 2 - stems: the dictionary part of the lexicon
#
# Features that belong to the word itself and hold for all its forms: which
# complements a verb takes, whether a noun can stand without a determiner, ...
# A word form finds its stem through its analysis in lexicon.txt
# (went -> go+ed -> the entry "go" below). Words without a suffix are their
# own stem. A stem may have several lines (do: auxiliary and main verb).
#
# Format:  stem   class   feature=value ...
#          class is a tag, or one of the shorthands defined here.
# A stem that needs no feature of its own (friend, the, historical) has no line.

V = VB VBP VBZ VBD VBG VBN
N = NN NNS
